Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Nile Coloring Books is a small shop from Cairo, Egypt, selling printable coloring books. Contact: sherif.in.26@gmail.com.

What this website collects

Almost nothing. This website does not run user accounts, does not ask for your name or address, and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The interactive coloring studio runs entirely in your browser — nothing you color is sent to us or stored anywhere.

Purchases

When you buy a book, you check out on Gumroad, our payment provider. Gumroad collects the payment and contact details needed to deliver your PDF and processes them under Gumroad's own privacy policy. We do not see or store your card details.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address only to answer you. We do not share it with anyone.

Children's privacy

This site is for parents and grown-ups buying books for kids. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
