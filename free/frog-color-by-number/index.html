Free Frog Color by Number (Ages 3–4)

A friendly frog on a lily pad, turned into a first color-by-number: just five big numbers to find — green for the frog's head, yellow for the sun, blue for the lily pad, orange for the feet and pink for the eyes — with a color key at the bottom of the page, so a 3- or 4-year-old can do the whole page alone. Print the free A4 PDF at home; to download it, a parent or guardian registers once with a name and email — then downloads are open.
