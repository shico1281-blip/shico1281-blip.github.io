Easy Coloring Pages for Kids

Just starting to color? These thirteen pages are the gentlest place to begin. Big shapes, thick outlines and lots of open space mean little hands can color inside the lines — and feel proud of the result. The frog, the turtle, the Nile fish, the Egyptian cat, the cow, the fox, the owl, the butterfly, the farm animals, the classroom, the beach, the ice cream and the train are all simple, friendly pictures with no fiddly details, so a first coloring session ends with a finished picture, not a frustrated artist.
