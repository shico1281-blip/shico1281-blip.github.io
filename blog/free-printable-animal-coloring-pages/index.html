10 Free Printable Animal Coloring Pages for Kids

Looking for a free activity that keeps little hands busy? These ten free printable animal coloring pages are made for kids ages 4 to 7, each one a friendly animal from the River Nile and ancient Egypt. Every page is a free A4 PDF: download it, print it at home, and color with crayons, pencils, or markers. No account, no sign-up, no email needed — pick a favorite below, or print all ten for a rainy afternoon.

1. Frog Coloring Page

Frogs love the green edges of the River Nile, hopping between reeds and lily pads. This friendly frog is waiting for bright leaf green, soft lime, or even a purple frog.

Fun fact for kids: a frog's long, sticky tongue can shoot out and catch a fly in the blink of an eye.

Download free PDF

2. Turtle Coloring Page

Turtles carry their homes on their backs, so they never have to hurry home for dinner. This gentle turtle's big shell is perfect for patterns — stripes, spots, swirls, or a rainbow.

Fun fact for kids: some turtles live over 100 years, and their shells are part of their bodies — they can never take them off.

Download free PDF

3. Lion Coloring Page

Lions are famous as kings of the wild, but this lion is friendlier than his roar sounds. His big mane frames his smiling face — try gold, warm orange, or brown.

Fun fact for kids: a lion's roar can be heard from 8 kilometers away, and lions are the only big cats that live in family groups, called prides.

Download free PDF

4. Elephant Coloring Page

Elephants are the biggest animals on land, and also some of the kindest. This gentle elephant waves hello with its trunk and listens with big, floppy ears.

Fun fact for kids: an elephant's trunk has around 40,000 muscles and can pick up a single tiny leaf — or a bunch of bananas!

Download free PDF

5. Nile Fish Coloring Page

The River Nile is full of fish, and tilapia is one of the most famous. This happy fish swims through river plants blowing bubbles — perfect for rainbow scales in blue, orange, and gold.

Fun fact for kids: fish breathe underwater through gills, which take tiny bits of air out of the water. Some Nile fish grow as long as your arm!

Download free PDF

6. Hippo Coloring Page

Hippos spend most of the day splashing in the river, with just their eyes, ears, and nose above the water. This happy hippo is having the best bath ever.

Fun fact for kids: a hippo's name means “river horse” in ancient Greek, and it can open its mouth wider than any other land animal.

Download free PDF

7. Crocodile Coloring Page

The Nile crocodile is one of the oldest animals on Earth, living along the river since the time of the pharaohs. This friendly crocodile rests on the sunny riverbank with a big toothy smile.

Fun fact for kids: a crocodile cannot stick out its tongue, and it can hold its breath underwater for more than an hour.

Download free PDF

8. Egyptian Cat Coloring Page

Cats were loved in ancient Egypt, treated like precious family members and painted on temple walls. This graceful cat sits tall with pointy ears and a curled tail — try sandy gold or calico patches.

Fun fact for kids: ancient Egyptians believed cats brought good luck, and the cat goddess Bastet protected homes and children.

Download free PDF

9. Pelican Coloring Page

Pelicans visit the River Nile with giant beaks and wide wings, gliding over the water like slow boats. This big bird stands proudly by the river, with a yellow or orange beak.

Fun fact for kids: a pelican's beak pouch can hold more fish than its whole belly can, and its wingspan can be wider than a grown-up is tall.

Download free PDF

10. Scarab Beetle Coloring Page

The scarab beetle was a special symbol in ancient Egypt, a sign of new beginnings. This shiny beetle shows off its round shell and patterned wings — try emerald green, blue, and gold.

Fun fact for kids: scarab beetles roll balls of earth many times bigger than themselves, pushing them with their back legs.

Download free PDF
