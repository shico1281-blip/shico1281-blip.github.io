Christmas Advent Countdown — 24 Days of Coloring

Christmas is coming! Open a new door every day from December 1 to December 24. Ten days hide a FREE coloring page to print at home — on the other days, meet our Christmas Mandala books — and every day has a short coloring video to watch.

How it works: 1. Come back every day — a new door opens. 2. On free days, print today’s page (A4, free) and color it your way. 3. Watch today’s Short and color along with us.

An honest note: there are 15 Shorts for 24 days, so 9 days repeat a Short — and only 5 of the 15 Shorts are Christmas-themed. Today’s Short is a coloring moment, not always the same picture as today’s page.
